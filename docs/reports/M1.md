## 8. Deviations & Uncertainties
- `AesGcm` constructor: Used `new AesGcm(key, tagSizeInBytes: 16)` due to obsolete parameterless constructor under .NET 10.
- `SupportedOSPlatform("windows")` added to `EncryptedFileVault` and DPAPI tests to satisfy analyzer `CA1416`.
- High-performance `[LoggerMessage]` used in `EncryptedFileVault` to satisfy `CA1848` and `CA1873`.
- Validation errors are presented inline in the Add/Edit `ContentDialog` using `dialog.Closing` cancellation (`args.Cancel = true;`), preventing dialogue dismissal while maintaining form state. Cookie is extracted directly into `SecretValue` and the PasswordBox is immediately wiped.
