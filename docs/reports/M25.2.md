# M25.2 & M25.3 Report — WPF BAML TypeConverter Fix & Global Crash Logger

## M25.2: WPF Baml2006 / TypeConverterMarkupExtension Fix
- **Root Cause**: `PublishTrimmed=true` stripped COM interop (`IBindCtx` in `FindMimeFromData`) and BAML type converter metadata, causing `System.Windows.Markup.XamlParseException: Provide value on 'System.Windows.Baml2006.TypeConverterMarkupExtension' threw an exception ---> System.TypeLoadException` during `MainWindow.InitializeComponent()`.
- **Fix**:
  - Removed trimming tags from `Directory.Build.props` and `src/App.Wpf/App.Wpf.csproj`.
  - Updated `src/App.Wpf/obfuscar.xml` to preserve XAML code-behind methods/fields on `App`, `MainWindow`, and `Views.*`.
  - Published with `-p:PublishSingleFile=true -p:IncludeAllContentForSelfExtract=true -p:PublishTrimmed=false -p:PublishReadyToRun=false`.

## M25.3: Global Exception Handler & `crash_log.txt`
- Registered and wired `WriteCrashLog` in `src/App.Wpf/App.xaml.cs` across:
  - `DispatcherUnhandledException` (UI thread)
  - `AppDomain.CurrentDomain.UnhandledException` (background threads)
  - `TaskScheduler.UnobservedTaskException` (unobserved tasks)
- Logs full timestamp, source, exception type, message, stack trace, and recursive `InnerException` chain to `Path.Combine(AppDomain.CurrentDomain.BaseDirectory, "crash_log.txt")`.
